Usage accounting.

Public usage stays prompt_tokens, completion_tokens, and total_tokens.
cachedContentTokenCount is a subset of the prompt count.
The attempt stores that subset as cache_read_input_tokens.
uncached_input_tokens is the prompt count minus the cached count.
thoughtsTokenCount is stored as thoughts_tokens.
Thinking text is not returned.
When Vertex omits totalTokenCount and thinking tokens are present, total_tokens stays null.
The proxy does not invent prompt plus visible output as the total.
