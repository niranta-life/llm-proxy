The canary is pinned first. Admission then runs on that deployment. An open breaker returns circuit_open. A hard budget below the quote returns budget_exceeded. A tenant rate limit returns tenant_rate_limited. None of those denials move the request to the baseline.

Provider, region, and data-policy mismatches hold the request on the baseline and leave the rollout active. The baseline still uses its own permissions and hard limits.
